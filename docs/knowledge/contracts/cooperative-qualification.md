## Work and accounting promises

One cumulative 600-second target starts at the **original first mandatory
effect**, including scheduler persistence, prepare, source capture/copies,
admission, workload, stops, wait attempts, finalization and delivery. No fresh
inner clock, later launch origin, added shutdown grace or assumed cross-clock
conversion is allowed. The prospective 560-second candidate, 530-second suite,
20-second stop and 2-second exit allocations must be explained from that same
origin, with prior work charged and overlapping allocations identified; they
are not additive allowances or OS guarantees.

Blocked syscalls, scheduling, fsync, positive waits/reaps and delivery may
outlast the target. Exhaustion stops qualification work through the supported
stop route and permanently prevents late PASS. It does not prove that stop
finished. Actual surviving parents retain unfinished child obligations;
storage/evidence owners retain accounting after timeout, report loss or turn
release. If an owner dies without a supported surviving actual-parent
relationship, custody stays unknown/FAILED/SPENT/retained. No perpetual parent,
portable wait-handle transfer or new cleanup authority is assumed.

**512 MiB is monitored owned scratch**, not an enforced aggregate physical
ceiling. The 384 MiB guard + 128 MiB outside decomposition is planning and
accounting *within* 512, neither enforced partitions nor free external space.
Inventory every actual root, capture, source, index, manifest, copy, log,
temporary publication, finalization and retained footprint across all providers,
including Nucleus DB/WAL/journal/results/output/evidence copies. Account
coexisting originals and copies and failure remnants. Retained evidence on
another backing remains charged after process closure. Under 5639v2 a
retained owned-image file is expected evidence, not failed attachment
closure; its bytes, metadata, copies and residual or unknown physical costs
remain accounted and disclosed. No physical reclamation, zero residual
allocation, physical-peak guarantee, free evidence backing or deletion
authority is claimed.

Record measurement scope, device, method, units, freshness, missing coverage
and sampled high-water marks. Samples establish observations at those times;
logical caps remain logical. A scheduler reservation establishes only its named
capacity, not reserved disk space or an unobserved peak. External APFS metadata,
COW, rounding and physical allocation unknowns must be disclosed, never zeroed
or hidden inside a fabricated finite reserve. The total physical peak is
deliberately unguaranteed. That disclosure may coexist with acceptance only
when every prerequisite to safe execution is positively supported: unknown
ownership, writer state, required scratch coverage or reserve safety refuses
before effects and fails/holds after effects. An unknown peak is never labelled
bounded.

Missing, stale or unreadable required samples, accounting overflow, a known
512 MiB monitored breach or capture overflow refuse before effects; after
effects they fail, request supported stop and retain unresolved obligations.
The 22 GiB start/abort threshold (20 GiB reserve + 2 GiB margin) remains
cooperative: refuse unknown/below-threshold admission and stop/report detected
loss, without claiming a reservation or protection from other writers.

## Independent success obligations

Bind exact published head/tree, ordered inputs, selector, actual run,
participant, authority, effective executable/argv/cwd/environment and original
clock origin. Preserve genuine Effigy admission on the **actual selector** and
attributable Effigy completion alongside complete raw capture. No synthetic
hook/token/environment, unknown provider or maintenance/later-admission
substitution qualifies. There is no 8 MiB truncation exemption, uncharged
second log, or silent redaction/compression/truncation presented as complete.
If supported machinery cannot retain full capture, the unresolved capability
is complete, durably acknowledged, source-bound raw delivery within monitored
accounting; relaxing resource promises supplies no implementation evidence.

A later route must name its **actual surviving direct-parent handle graph**
before effects. For a directly execed actor the reported conditional graph is
invoker → Effigy owner → actor → actor-created custodian → monitor/leaves;
shells, supervisors and publishers add their actual creation edges. The
publisher's actual creator must retain its wait relationship too. This is an
obligation map, not an implemented surviving graph. Every required direct
parent must positively wait/reap its exact required direct children, including
actor, custodian, monitor, leaves and publisher. Identities, capacity holds,
EOF and empty-group samples transfer no handle. Never group-KILL the last
custodian. Owner loss cannot invent a controller, subreaper or successor.

Require separately and conjunctively:

- actual surviving creator-owned positive waits and reaps of every required
  direct child;
- qualified, completed, JOINED, custodian-bound **DECIDING** census;
- a separate **FINAL** certain-empty census for the exact closure group;
- complete raw capture with real EOF, drain, successful fsync, source
  binding, in-window durable acknowledgement and publisher wait;
- genuine actual Effigy admission on the actual selector;
- where the proof owns an image, separate ordinary provider evidence that
  **every mount and attached entity or device of each exact owned image is
  absent**, while the retained image file remains expected counted evidence.

None replaces actual waits or another predicate. A successful detach command
or an absent mount alone is insufficient. A leftover unmounted, container or
synthesized entity, an incomplete inventory, an unreadable binding or an
unknown provider result cannot pass. This is a required future provider
contract, not a claim that current code supplies it; no provider, scan,
cleanup controller or capability is invented here.

5639v2 prospectively supersedes the earlier literal entire-backing-object
and residual-allocation absence gate. Directory deletion, removed-container
flags and empty groups still prove neither attachment absence nor storage
reclamation. The retained file never authorizes erasing other evidence; all
retained storage stays accounted.

PASS requires every applicable source, **445-case**, oracle, admission,
capture, EOF, drain, successful fsync, binding, acknowledgement, publisher-wait,
deciding-join, final-census, whole-attachment-absence and retained-and-counted
image-evidence obligation, with no missing, partial, timed-out or late
required evidence. Report time-budget outcome, workload
outcome, evidence completeness, measured resources and closure separately.
FAILED/SPENT/unknown and the original first fault remain permanent even after
later cleanup. Unknown or retained work blocks dependent occurrences and
lifecycle operations until separately supported reconciliation. No automatic
retry, refund, clock reset, fresh occurrence or cleanup follows.

### One 600-second origin across every phase

Origin t0 is immediately before the attempt's **original first mandatory
effect** — reservation persistence included — and is established by
`DispatchLaunch.proofExecution` as `{clockId, originTick, targetMs: 600000}`
(`shared/schema/review/proof.ts:27`). It is never restarted at a stage, a
submitted command, a supervisor launch or a helper's process start.

| Phase (all inside the one target) | Bound and seam | Why the local clock alone is not the supplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scheduler/reservation persistence | before t0's successor work; t0 is chosen before it | Nucleus's `originTick` is a local elapsed tick, not the common binding (its own comment says so) |
| prepare | Effigy's prepare phase, clipped to what remains | Effigy currently opens its own deadline at process start |
| source capture/copies | Nucleus source/tree binding and any evidence copy | copies may precede the submit; they are charged to the same origin |
| admission | Effigy hook/admission wait, capped at the inherited ceiling minus Effigy's settlement allowance | a queue wait must not create a new span |
| workload | the suite's candidate window (560 s) and the suite's own alarm (530 s) | today both are measured from their own starts |
| stop / wait / reap | the guard's 20 s stop reserve and 2 s exit margin inside its `--limit` (`stop_at = absolute − 20`, `exit_at = absolute − 2`) | these are reserves inside one window, never additive allowances |
| finalization + durable delivery | the endpoint's ack and the service's S wait and settlement | a late ack cannot recover PASS |

Allocation arithmetic against the same origin: the candidate cutoff is t0+560,
the suite cutoff t0+530, the stop allocation 20 s and the exit allocation 2 s
fit **inside** the candidate's remaining window (560 − 20 = 540 stop, 560 − 2
= 558 exit), not beside it; the guard's setup bound (10 s) is likewise inside
its own limit. Overlap is explicit — no stage gets a fresh span, and no
allocation is summed with another. Blocked syscalls, scheduling, fsync, waits,
reaps and delivery may outlast the target; hitting it permanently fails
qualification and leaves the surviving parents' obligations with them.

**Current clock interface: consumer binding unsupported.** The former proposal
`{originId, elapsedAtSpawnMs, remainingMs}` plus `sinceStartMs` is withdrawn as
an agreed interface: Effigy's reconciled 038 explicitly refuses duration-chain
reconstruction. It is not permission to replace the original clock.

Effigy's actual internal payload is `deadline_nanos: u64`, absolute nanoseconds
on `libc::CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, with **no origin identity**. Its
`EFFIGY_RUN_DEADLINE_MS` input is a duration from Effigy's own process start,
not an inherited absolute deadline (`run_deadline.rs:28–29,80–89,110,239–246`;
`supervise/memory.rs:93,140–159`). `from_origin` computes the nanos ceiling
from its own current read plus the duration; an injected origin does not bind
that value to an earlier origin. Phase readbacks are diagnostics, not authority.

Neither payload covers Nucleus reservation persistence, source capture or other
effects before Effigy starts. A prepare sub-origin may only narrow an inherited
ceiling, never mint or restart the full allowance. MS startup, worker spawn,
gate and record effects are charged from the original origin even on refusal;
"no prepare-root writes" does not mean no effects or allocations.

The original-origin consumer binding and predecessor coverage remain
**UNSUPPORTED, refuse** until their owners supply a source-bound same-timebase
control. No `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW` ↔ `Instant`/`CLOCK_MONOTONIC` conversion is
assumed, and no clock service or inner clock is introduced. An allowance that
cannot fit is a blocker, never a reset.

Effigy's sentinel is **MS**; S above remains the Nucleus supervisor/publisher.
MS holds no wait handle for its siblings W or the selector leader. Its gate or
pin settlement proves neither their completion nor E's reap. Expired or
abandoned `try_wait` leaves the edge unknown; every required edge still needs
its actual creator's positive wait receipt with both identities, status,
time/source and subject/run/attempt binding. 038 marks absent receipt fields
**MISSING**. A sibling terminal or EOF cannot fill the publisher's own wait,
and later exclusion settlement cannot recover qualification credit.

### DECIDING and FINAL: reuse the qualified provider, refuse the candidates

Both census inputs reuse the **ordinary qualified guard** (`scripts/bounded-test.py`,
sha256 `fcfda2b0a43afd192a4f75e0d575b9aa94f0fd79d195bd75b1c66305f37f046b`)
and nothing else:

- **DECIDING** is `receipt.deciding_census` / `deciding_census_seq`: the exact
  monitor message the custodian's echoed decision named, resolved against the
  monitor's own record of what it sent (`bind_deciding_census`), with
  `{certain, members, uncertain}` plus its sequence.
- **FINAL** is the separate last completed round (`receipt.census_join.final`,
  from `census_last`), recorded after the deciding message, with its own tick;
  `census_settled(join, final, deciding)` requires all three facts separately
  and refuses a frozen prefix, a decided-but-not-final round and any nonempty
  or uncertain round.
- **JOIN** is `receipt.census_join` (`joined`, `started`, `bound_s`,
  `waited_ns`, `rows`, `deciding`, `final`, `frozen`, `refused`), bounded by
  `CENSUS_JOIN_S = 3.0` and never past the deadline.

Neither census is a wait, and neither substitutes for one: an empty group, EOF,
a PID or a terminal row proves no census, and a certain-empty census proves no
wait. The parked 233 candidate `c578…`, the unpublished ceiling candidate
`4aba…` and its clipped `join_census` are **unqualified** and no observation
from them may be read into these inputs. Residual, stated plainly: both facts
come from one monitor thread, so FINAL's independence is from DECIDING, the
join and every wait — not from the monitor process. A later need for a
separate final-only census authority is an owner decision, not a change made
here.

### Image attachment: Tom 5639v2 applied to the exact bindings

For each exact owned image, the gate is satisfied only by positive ordinary
provider evidence that **every mount and every attached entity/device is
absent**, while the image **file** stays present and charged. The ordinary
provider is the same qualified guard's post-census route, not a new component:

- the proof publishes one bounded atomic `detach-request.json` naming the
  mounted entity's own backing, mount, `device` and `volume_uuid`
  (`DETACH_REQUEST_NAME`, `detach_request_problems`); the device is the
  **mounted** entity's, because an APFS image attaches as a container plus a
  synthesized whole disk;
- only on a `cleanup` decision does `post_census_detach` re-derive every
  binding from kernel and `hdiutil` facts, under the custodian's own remaining
  wait window, and make at most **one ordinary** detach within that budget;
- success is conjunctive: exit zero, no spawn error, no timeout, no withdrawn
  authority, the child positively reaped, **and** the mount gone with **no
  entity of that backing still attached**; an unmounted volume, a leftover
  container or a synthesized disk is not a detach;
- a request-free proof may still publish `image-custody.json` before attaching;
  `custody_problems` then keeps the root when a backing is mounted, attached
  without a mount point, or uncertain, and a stale `hdiutil` entry whose device
  node is positively gone keeps nothing;
- facts fail closed: a missing/non-list image list, an image without a path, an
  empty entity list, an entity without a device, a non-string mount or UUID, or
  a declared backing with no entities is **unreadable**, never "positively
  clear".

This is exactly Tom's gate and it needs no new probe: attaching with ownership
enabled, observing the mount read-only, and reading the post-state are already
the suite's own behaviour. The design does not inspect any image or historical
resource; it names the ordinary provider and its exact facts. A fresh
composition still needs its own later native ladder, and the retained file's
bytes, metadata, copies and residual/unknown physical cost remain charged.

### Capture and durable delivery: one NQRAW001 artifact

The composition is already the conjunction P4/P6 implements, and it must stay
one artifact:

- S is the sole writer of one mode-0600 `qualification.raw`: header
  `NQRAW001`, then 22-byte big-endian frames (`kind`, `stream`, global
  `sequence`, per-stream `offset`, `length`), DATA 1–65,536 bytes in short-write
  loops, exactly one EOF frame per stream on its **real** pipe EOF and exactly
  one DRAIN after that stream's completed payload writes (empty streams
  included). Size identity is `8 + B + 22 × (N + 4)`.
- `close()` fsyncs the artifact and reports file, directory-publication and
close faults strictly; a latched fault keeps the partial artifact and the
settlement discloses partial accounting rather than claiming fit.
- The endpoint validates framing and subject, compares decoded per-stream
counts/hashes with S's observed counts/hashes and its real EOF/DRAIN
observations, then durably acknowledges those exact facts, the observation
digest and its own acceptance tick inside the original target
(`qualification.ack.json`).
- The service then positively waits S and writes
`qualification.settlement.json`; intake consumes that exact settlement digest
through the executor's existing authentication and refers to the single
artifact, never a `proofFiles` copy.

No second full log, no 8 MiB exemption, no redaction/compression/truncation, no
manufactured EOF from pipe destruction and no completion inferred from exit
zero. Qualification mode therefore opens only the one file; the ordinary
stdout/stderr paths, token redaction and per-stream cap stay exactly as they
are for every non-qualification run. The remaining supplier gaps are wiring the
publisher's real creator wait into the settlement (service→S is P2 and is
recorded; any Effigy/Oxide publisher adds its own edge) and the Effigy run
receipt above.

### Accounting inventory under monitored 512 MiB

512 MiB is **monitored owned scratch**, not an enforced aggregate physical
ceiling; 384 MiB workload + 128 MiB evidence is planning *within* that 512, and
there is no free outside part. Every creator's footprint is charged:

| Creator / footprint | Accounting boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Nucleus database, WAL, ordered journal, scheduler records, partial writes | charge the whole shared footprint when per-run attribution is unproven; WAL/FULL config reserves nothing |
| host-run files: submission/env, plan, live state, cancel, disconnect, result, retention, the three qualification sidecars | owned paths; temporary **and** final bytes, plus publication remnants |
| `qualification.raw` including framing, partial frames and any retained failure copy | actual encoded bytes; `st_blocks * 512` per distinct `(device, inode)`, no symlink follow |
| upstream pipe and delivery buffers | declared coverage only; kernel buffer occupancy is not owned scratch |
| Effigy state: `.effigy/state`, `kernel.db` + sidecars, terminal ring (16,448 B), per-task gate bodies (4,096 B each), `.scope` markers, capture buffers | Effigy owner's disclosure; conflicting shares charged once by the owning entry |
| Oxide guard: owned root, evidence directory, census/settlement records, scratch pool slots, staged target | the guard's own `owned-bytes` walk (`st_blocks × 512`, distinct inode) sampled with a completion stamp |
| the owned 128 MiB test image **file** and its APFS metadata/COW | retained and charged; physical allocation unknown and disclosed, never zeroed |
| suite fixture temp roots, Cargo target/work, failure remnants | per-run roots inside the monitored root; retained on failure |

The 22 GiB threshold (20 GiB reserve + 2 GiB margin) is cooperative: unknown
or below-threshold admission refuses before effects; detected loss stops and
reports. Samples establish only their observation times; methods, units,
device, freshness, missing coverage and sampled high-water marks are recorded,
and the total physical peak stays deliberately unguaranteed. No current write
is excluded, and no synthetic reserve makes fit true.

### Nucleus-owned prerequisite: exiting a retained failure row

P7 keeps a spent qualification's raw bytes, sidecars and charged residuals
while anything is unresolved, and the settlement's only spent disposition is
the literal `unknown_SPENT_retained` with `retention.keep: true`
(`hostRunQualificationDispositionSchema`). There is **no owner-supported exit**
for that row after closure is separately reconciled: no verb releases it, no
disposition records a resolved closure, and ordinary expiry is explicitly not
deletion authority. Before any real proof, the Nucleus owner must supply that
exit within its own lifecycle authority. This design invents no verb, cleanup
controller or deletion authority, and a missing exit keeps the row retained.

## Disposition and premise coverage

The Nucleus route proposal, any separately scoped reviewed implementation and
accepted source and actual fit/readiness must precede a separately activated
adequate uncounted **445-case diagnostic**,
counted author, distinct retained reviewer and final exact-head disposition,
before 309 publication → 061 → 304. No ladder step is waived or activated.
Old receipts, grants, failures and clocks stay bound to their original bytes.
Unpublished/unvalidated local 309 source is ineligible. Ordinary guard `fcfda`,
parked 233 `c578`, candidate `4aba` and suite `b8d445` stay unchanged, including
the published 309 `fc6` suite's positive detach oracle that the 8 MiB image
file remain (`scripts/test_supervisor_ceiling_qualification.py:10585–10596`).
All three Barge and seven Oxide historical OPEN/UNCLOSED image resources remain
untouched; Rust 1.100 stays unqualified and 083 negative-only.

The supplier design above adds no provider, no wiring and no activation, and
its Nucleus-owned retained-failure exit blocks any real proof until that owner
supplies it. Ordinary guard `fcfda`, the parked 233 candidate, the ceiling
candidate and suite `b8d445` remain byte-unchanged by this design. All nine
[workflow premises](workflow.md#proof-authoring-premise-checklist)
are **not-executable-docs** here: (1) private HOME/rustup, (2) account-home
control, (3) environment isolation, (4) automatic adoption, (5) Cargo external
argv, (6) owned images, (7) compiler flags, (8) temporary storage and

apply them to actual bodies/helpers. Documentation validation is no native
evidence. Implementation readiness remains **false**.
