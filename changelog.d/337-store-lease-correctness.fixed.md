Store and lease-core corrections (oxide#337). A removable volume's reserve is now applied only to the removable pool and read from its paired internal control; a failed slot attempt removes its wait ticket; crash recovery re-locates its record after a reload; pool creation no longer reports success for a failure other than the already-initialized race; and the writer-update refusal is consumed once instead of poisoning later commands on the same thread. A deferred idle eviction whose ledger save fails is rolled back instead of leaving a hand-off record naming a live row, the settlement pass validates its pool read-only before locking it and reports a Barge custody failure as such, and one protection listing now decides every victim of a byte-pressure loop. Emptying `idle-evictions.json` is synced, private directories are created owner-only in one step, and an abandoned-hold lock probe is no longer counted as a byte walk.
