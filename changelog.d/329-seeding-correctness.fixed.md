Cold-slot seeding now bounds donor work by catalogs rather than idle slots, so a seeded recipient or a slot without a catalog no longer crowds a real donor out of the eight-catalog read. An interrupted sidecar write's temp file no longer counts against the 24 MiB sidecar family and an old one is reaped. A seed whose every clone has verified is no longer refused on a small work-deadline overrun. Library plan checks refuse any unit outside the v1 scope, and catalog validation now requires a unit's target and its 16-hex identifier to agree across the fingerprint directory, artifacts and `.d`. A `[lib]` manifest's `crate-type` is honoured, an rmeta-only unit from a check-built donor is offered whole, and selection reports a reason instead of a bare miss.
